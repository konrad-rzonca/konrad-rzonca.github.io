# Blind Judging

A judge leans toward whatever its prompt hints at: your favorite, an earlier verdict, a measurement. Give a fresh subagent only the candidates, shuffled and labeled only by letter, each shown where a reader would meet it, and ask what each one means or which works best.

One judge decides. When many readers will meet the choice, or it is costly to change later, use up to three and word the brief differently for each. If they agree, that decides. If they split, ask the user.
